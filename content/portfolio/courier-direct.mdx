## Overview

CourierDirect is a logistics-facing web product centered on parcel tracking and operational clarity — the surfaces operators and customers use to know where a shipment is, and the supporting pages that explain services and contact paths.

Scope here is lighter than Trackify: a focused web delivery rather than a full multi-year product memoir.

## Problem

Courier operations fail in the gaps — when tracking is hard to find, status language is vague, or the public site and ops tools feel like two different companies. The work needed a clear tracking entry point and a coherent marketing/ops shell around it.

## Role

Web and product UI: hero and tracking entry, service storytelling, and the visual system that keeps logistics trust signals (status, contact, next step) readable under pressure.

## Decisions

- **Tracking first** — put “track your parcel” in the first viewport with a direct number field, not buried under brochure copy.
- **Ops language, calm chrome** — status and contact affordances stay obvious; avoid decorative card stacks that slow scanning.
- **Service + FAQs as support** — secondary pages answer how shipping works without competing with the tracking job.
- **Responsive logistics UI** — the track flow and contact paths must work on a phone in a warehouse doorway as well as on a desk.

## Outcome

A live courier web presence with a prominent tracking path and supporting service/about content. Use **Visit site** for the current production experience.
