## Overview

[falanocollege.com](https://falanocollege.com) is an educational resource portal for students who need notes, syllabi, practice questions, and past papers in one place — across Science, Management, Humanities, and Law.

I owned the web platform craft: structure, visual system, and a publishable surface that stays readable on phone and desktop.

## Problem

Study resources for Nepali college tracks are often scattered across PDFs, group chats, and outdated pages. Students waste time hunting for the right syllabus or old paper instead of studying. The product needed a calm browse path by discipline — not a noisy content dump.

## Role

End-to-end web delivery: information architecture for course families, landing and browse patterns, and a maintainable content model so materials and news can ship without redesigning the shell each time.

## Decisions

- **Discipline-first IA** — Science, Management, Humanities, and Law as primary doors, so students land in a familiar track before drilling into notes or papers.
- **Resource clarity over novelty** — prioritize findable syllabi, notes, and past papers; resist feature sprawl that would bury the studying job.
- **Editorial + news surface** — a lightweight news lane for guides and updates without turning the home composition into a magazine grid.
- **Honest stack** — WordPress-backed publishing where content velocity matters more than a custom CMS, with a designed front that does not look like a default theme.

## Outcome

A live educational portal students can open today. Browse by course family, read materials, and follow newer guides from the same home. Use **Visit site** for the current production experience.
