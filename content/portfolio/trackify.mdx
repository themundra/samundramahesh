## Overview

Trackify is a privacy-first finance tracker I built with Flutter: encrypted local storage, optional Firestore sync, and concise analytics for weekly trends and category breakdowns.

I designed and shipped the product end-to-end — information architecture, UI, local persistence, sync paths, and the analytics surfaces that make weekly spending readable at a glance.

## Problem

Most finance apps push accounts to the cloud before they earn trust. I wanted a tool that starts local, keeps balances clear across cash and digital methods, and only syncs when the user chooses.

## Approach

- **Encrypted local store** as the default source of truth
- **Multi-method balances** so cash, bank, and wallets stay distinct
- **Responsive charts** for weekly trends and category breakdowns
- **Optional Firestore sync** for users who want cross-device continuity

## Role

Primary developer — app architecture, storage layer, analytics UI, and release packaging for Android APK distribution.
