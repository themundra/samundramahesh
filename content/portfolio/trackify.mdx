## Overview

Trackify is a privacy-first personal finance tracker built with Flutter. It features encrypted local storage, optional cloud sync, and analytics for weekly trends and category breakdowns — with privacy and user experience as top priorities.

I designed and shipped the product end-to-end: information architecture, UI, local persistence, sync paths, and the visualization surfaces that make spending readable at a glance.

## Problem

Most finance apps push accounts to the cloud before they earn trust. Trackify starts local: balances stay clear across cash and digital methods, and sync is opt-in rather than the center of the product.

## Key features

- **Encrypted local storage** — financial data encrypted on-device with industry-standard protection
- **Optional cloud sync** — Firebase when you want cross-device continuity, or fully local for maximum privacy
- **Multi-method tracking** — cash, cards, digital wallets, and bank transfers
- **Weekly analytics** — trend analysis with charts for spending patterns and categories
- **Accessible interface** — built with accessibility best practices
- **Category management** — custom categories with detailed breakdowns

## Technology

Flutter and Dart for the client, Hive for lightweight local storage, Firebase for optional sync and auth, AES-256 oriented encryption for data security, and FL Chart for visualization.
