Most finance apps ask for an account before they earn trust. Trackify flips that order.

The first version stores balances and transactions on-device with encryption. Charts stay fast because they read from a local model. Firestore sync exists, but it is optional — a continuity feature, not a gate.

That decision shaped everything else: multi-method balances (cash vs digital), weekly trend views that stay readable, and an APK-first distribution path for people who want to try the product without a store account dance.

If you are shipping a Flutter utility, start with the trust boundary. Local-first is not nostalgia — it is product clarity.
