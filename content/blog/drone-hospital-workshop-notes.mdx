Drone Hospital Nepal is a workshop line, not a second brand on this site. The useful details are operational.

**Repair** means diagnosis before parts: hardware faults, electronics, firmware and calibration issues, motors and props — across common consumer platforms and custom builds. Guessing the board wastes a day; asking how you fly saves one.

**Maintenance** is preventive: performance checks, cleaning, calibration, battery health. Operators who only show up after a crash pay twice.

**Training** stays practical. Beginners and field users need drills, safety habits, and regulations awareness — including CAAN-oriented preparation when certification is the goal — not slide decks that never leave the room.

Consultation sits in front of purchase and fleet growth: what to buy, what to maintain, what to retire. Kathmandu is the workshop base; remote guidance is possible when the aircraft and symptoms are clear enough to triage without opening every panel on day one.

If you need repair, a maintenance plan, or a training block, say which — the next step is clearer when the job has a name.
