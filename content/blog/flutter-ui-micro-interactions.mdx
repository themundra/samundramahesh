Micro-interactions fail when they decorate instead of explain.

On mobile, a good motion usually does one of three jobs: confirm an action, reveal hierarchy, or bridge a state change the user initiated. Everything else competes with content.

In practice I keep a tight budget — often two or three intentional motions per surface — and I test them with reduced-motion preferences in mind. If the UI still reads without the animation, the motion is allowed to be delightful. If it does not, the interaction design is unfinished.

This portfolio follows the same rule on the web: route enter, nav indicator, work reveal. No glow stacks. No motion for its own sake.
